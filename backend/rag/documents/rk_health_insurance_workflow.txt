# ClaimCare - Complete Claim Workflow

ClaimCare is an AI-assisted health insurance claim assistance project.

The application provides a workflow for submitting, checking and tracking health insurance claims.

General workflow:

1. Claim Submission
   The patient submits available claim information and supporting documents.

2. Claim Recording
   The application records the submitted claim information and can generate or associate a Claim ID.

3. Document Verification
   Uploaded documents can be checked for availability, readability and consistency with the submitted claim information.

4. Eligibility Checking
   Available policy, claim and treatment information can be used for a preliminary eligibility-related check.

5. Fraud-Risk Screening
   The application can perform automated fraud-risk screening to identify records that may require additional review.

6. External Insurer Workflow
   If an external insurance provider is selected, the application can prepare claim information and supporting documents for insurer review through the configured workflow.

7. Insurer Review
   The applicable insurance provider or authorized reviewer evaluates the claim according to the applicable policy and review process.

8. Claim Status
   The resulting or current claim status can be recorded and displayed in the ClaimCare application.

9. Claim Tracking
   The patient can use the Claim ID to track the recorded claim status.

Important limitations:

- The AI assistant provides informational guidance only.
- The AI assistant cannot approve or reject claims.
- The AI assistant cannot guarantee reimbursement.
- The AI assistant cannot guarantee coverage.
- The AI assistant cannot guarantee processing time.
- The AI assistant cannot invent policy-specific insurance rules.
- Automated fraud-risk screening is not proof of fraud.
- Final claim decisions belong to the applicable insurer or authorized reviewer.

ClaimCare is the project/application name used in this knowledge base.
