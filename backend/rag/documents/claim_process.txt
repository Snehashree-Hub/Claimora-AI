# ClaimCare - Claim Process

Purpose:
This document describes the claim-processing workflow implemented or represented by the ClaimCare project.

Claim submission:
A patient submits claim information through the ClaimCare claim workflow. Submitted information may include patient details, policy details, treatment information, claim amount and supporting documents.

After submission:
1. The submitted claim information is recorded by the application.
2. A Claim ID can be generated or associated with the claim according to the application workflow.
3. Uploaded supporting documents can be checked.
4. An eligibility check can be performed using the information available to the application.
5. Fraud-risk screening can be performed as an additional automated review.
6. For an external insurer workflow, claim information and supporting documents can be prepared for review by the selected insurance provider.
7. The applicable insurer or authorized reviewer makes the final claim decision.
8. The recorded claim status can be displayed in the ClaimCare dashboard.
9. The patient can use the Claim ID to track the claim.

Important:
The ClaimCare AI assistant provides informational guidance. It does not guarantee claim approval, rejection, reimbursement amount, coverage, or processing time.

Actual insurance coverage, policy conditions and final claim decisions depend on the applicable insurance policy and authorized insurer or reviewer.
