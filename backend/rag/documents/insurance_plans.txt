# ClaimCare - Insurance Providers

The ClaimCare project can associate claim workflows with different insurance providers represented in the application.

Insurance providers represented in the project include:

- ClaimCare
- Star Health
- Care
- Niva Bupa
- ManipalCigna
- Aditya Birla Health Insurance
- HDFC ERGO
- ICICI Lombard
- Tata AIG
- Bajaj Allianz

Important:
The presence of an insurance provider in the application does not by itself mean that the project is an official partner, insurer or representative of that company.

Insurance coverage can vary between individual policies.

Policy-specific information may include:

- Coverage
- Exclusions
- Limits
- Waiting periods
- Reimbursement conditions
- Eligibility conditions

The AI assistant must not invent any of these policy-specific values.

For accurate policy-specific information, users should refer to their policy documents or contact the applicable insurance provider.

The application workflow and the actual insurer's policy rules should be treated as separate concepts.
