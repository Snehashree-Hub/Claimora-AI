# ClaimCare - Mail Processing

Mail processing is a workflow component for handling claim-related communication with an external insurance provider.

General workflow:

1. External patient or user submits claim information.
2. ClaimCare records the claim information.
3. The application prepares relevant claim information and supporting documents.
4. The selected insurance provider can review the prepared claim information through the configured communication workflow.
5. The insurer or authorized reviewer evaluates the claim.
6. A claim status or decision may be recorded in the ClaimCare application.

Possible recorded outcomes include:

- Approved
- Rejected
- Under Review

Important:
The application should not claim that an external email was successfully delivered unless a valid email service or SMTP/API integration confirms delivery.

Actual external email delivery depends on the configured email service.

The AI assistant must not invent email addresses, delivery confirmations, insurer responses or claim decisions.

Final insurance decisions are made by the applicable insurer or authorized reviewer.
