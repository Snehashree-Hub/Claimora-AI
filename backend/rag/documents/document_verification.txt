# ClaimCare - Document Verification

Document verification is a step in the ClaimCare claim workflow.

The purpose of document verification is to check whether submitted claim documents are available and suitable for processing by the application workflow.

Examples of supporting documents may include:

- Hospital bills
- Discharge summaries
- Prescriptions
- Diagnostic reports
- Medical records
- Treatment-related documents

The application may consider information such as:

- Whether a document was uploaded
- Whether the document can be read or processed
- Whether required information appears to be present
- Whether the document information is consistent with the submitted claim information

If a document is missing, unreadable or inconsistent, additional information or manual review may be required.

Important:
Document verification does not automatically mean that an insurance claim will be approved.

The AI assistant must not claim that a document is legally valid or that an insurer is required to accept it. Final document acceptance and claim decisions depend on the applicable insurer or authorized reviewer.
