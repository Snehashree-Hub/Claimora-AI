# ClaimCare - Claim Tracking

Claim tracking allows a patient to check the status of a submitted claim using the Claim ID associated with the claim.

Claim ID:
The Claim ID is used by the ClaimCare claim workflow to identify and track a submitted claim.

Possible workflow statuses include:

- Submitted
- Document Verification
- Eligibility Checking
- Fraud Risk Screening
- Insurer Review
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected

The exact status shown depends on the current application workflow and the status recorded for that claim.

Approved and Rejected are claim outcomes, not processing steps.

Important:
A displayed status should not be interpreted as a guaranteed future outcome. Final claim decisions are made by the applicable insurer or authorized reviewer.

The AI assistant can explain the meaning of available claim statuses but cannot change a claim status or guarantee an outcome.
