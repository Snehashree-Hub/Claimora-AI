# ClaimCare - Eligibility Checking

Eligibility checking is a preliminary step in the ClaimCare claim workflow.

The application can use available claim, policy and treatment information to perform an eligibility-related check.

Information that may be relevant includes:

- Policy information
- Treatment information
- Claim information
- Coverage information available to the application
- Other applicable insurance information

The eligibility check is intended to provide preliminary workflow information.

Important:
An eligibility result from the application does not guarantee:

- Claim approval
- Reimbursement
- Coverage
- Payment
- Final insurer acceptance

Insurance policies can contain different terms, exclusions, limits and conditions. The AI assistant must not invent policy-specific rules.

The final determination of eligibility and coverage belongs to the applicable insurance provider or authorized reviewer.

Patients should provide accurate information when submitting a claim.
