# ClaimCare - Fraud Risk Screening

Fraud-risk screening is an additional review mechanism in the ClaimCare claim workflow.

The purpose of fraud-risk screening is to identify claim records that may contain patterns or information that require additional examination.

The screening system may consider characteristics such as:

- Unusual claim patterns
- Inconsistent claim information
- Data characteristics identified by the fraud-risk model
- Other risk indicators available to the application

Important:

A high-risk result does not prove that fraud has occurred.

A fraud-risk flag does not automatically mean that:

- The patient committed fraud
- The claim will be rejected
- The patient will be denied coverage

A fraud-risk result should be treated as an indicator for additional review.

The final claim decision belongs to the applicable insurer or authorized reviewer.

The AI assistant should explain fraud-risk screening carefully and must not accuse a patient of fraud based only on an automated risk result.
